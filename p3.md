# Practical 3: Regulatory Compliance in Data Privacy

## Aim

To study important data protection laws and understand
how organizations comply with privacy regulations.

## Introduction

Regulatory compliance means following laws, rules,
and guidelines designed to protect personal information.

Organizations collect personal data such as names,
email addresses, phone numbers, and location details.
They must handle this information responsibly.

## Important Data Protection Regulations

### 1. GDPR

GDPR stands for General Data Protection Regulation.

It is a data protection law applicable in the European
Union and certain situations involving people in the EU.

Important principles:
- Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimization
- Accuracy
- Storage limitation
- Integrity and confidentiality
- Accountability

### 2. Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

The DPDP Act is India's law dealing with the processing
of digital personal data.

Important concepts include:
- Data Principal
- Data Fiduciary
- Consent
- Notice
- Personal data
- Data protection obligations

The exact commencement and applicability of provisions
should be checked against current official notifications.

### 3. CCPA

CCPA stands for California Consumer Privacy Act.

It provides privacy rights to eligible California
consumers regarding their personal information.

## Comparison

| Feature | GDPR | DPDP Act | CCPA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Region | European Union | India | California, USA |
| Main focus | Personal data protection | Digital personal data | Consumer privacy |
| Consent | Important legal basis | Important requirement | Notice and consumer rights |
| Individual rights | Access, correction, erasure, etc. | Rights under the Act | Access, deletion, correction, etc. |

## Practical Activity

Consider an online shopping application that collects:

- Name
- Email address
- Mobile number
- Delivery address
- Payment-related information

The organization should:

1. Inform users about data collection.
2. Collect only necessary information.
3. Use data for stated purposes.
4. Protect stored information.
5. Provide applicable user rights.
6. Follow relevant legal requirements.

## Observation

Data protection regulations help organizations
handle personal information responsibly and
increase user trust.

## Result

Studied major data protection regulations and
understood the importance of regulatory compliance.

## Conclusion

Regulatory compliance is essential for protecting
personal data and ensuring responsible data processing.

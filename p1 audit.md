# Practical 1: Data Privacy Audit of W3Schools

## Aim
To conduct a data privacy audit of the W3Schools website and identify how user data is collected, used, stored, and protected.

## Website Selected
- **Organization:** W3Schools
- **Website:** [W3Schools](https://www.w3schools.com)
- **Privacy Policy:** [W3Schools Privacy Policy](https://www.w3schools.com/about/about_privacy.asp)

## Requirements
- Internet connection
- Web browser
- W3Schools account (optional)

## Procedure
1. Visit the W3Schools website.
2. Open its Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.
3. Identify the types of personal information collected.
4. Check how the information is used.
5. Examine cookies and tracking technologies.
6. Check third-party services and data sharing.
7. Examine available privacy and security controls.
8. Identify possible privacy risks.
9. Suggest measures to improve privacy.

## Observations

| Privacy Aspect | Observation | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Data Collection | Name, email, phone number, location, billing address, IP address, device details and usage data may be collected. | Medium |
| Data Usage | Data is used to run, improve and support the services, process payments, ensure security, and for marketing. | Low |
| Cookies | Cookies and similar technologies are used for functionality, analytics and advertising. | Medium |
| Advertising | Free users see targeted ads, and advertising partners use persistent identifiers that can recognize a device across websites. | High |
| Third-party Services | Data is shared with service providers such as Amazon, Google, Stripe, PayPal and Zendesk, and with advertising partners. | Medium |
| Consent Control | A consent tool lets users accept or refuse analytics and personalized ads, and withdraw consent at any time. | Low |
| Security | Administrative, technical and physical safeguards are used, but the policy says no measure is completely foolproof. | Medium |
| Data Retention | Data is kept for the "minimum period necessary", with no specific time stated. | Medium |
| Data Deletion | Account and related data are stated to be deleted within 30 days of deletion, subject to exceptions. | Low |

> These observations are based on W3Schools' published Privacy Policy (last updated 16 September 2026).

## Privacy Risks Identified
- Advertising identifiers can track user activity across other websites.
- Signed-in free users who consent to personalized ads may have an identifier created from their email address.
- Some advertising partners decide independently how they use data, so users must exercise their rights with each of them separately.
- Retention periods are not clearly defined.
- Data may be transferred and processed outside the user's country.
- W3Schools states it is not responsible for how its sub-processors handle data beyond what it shares with them.

## Recommendations
- Use the "Manage Consent" option to refuse personalized advertising.
- Browse without signing in where possible, since an account is not required to access tutorials.
- Review and clear cookies regularly, or use tracker-blocking tools.
- Avoid sharing unnecessary personal information in profiles or support messages.
- Review the permissions and policies of third-party services linked from the site.
- Read the Privacy Policy before using additional services such as Spaces, certifications or the mobile app.

## Result
The Data Privacy Audit of W3Schools was successfully performed. The audit identified its data collection, usage, cookies, advertising, third-party sharing, security measures and potential privacy risks.

## Conclusion
W3Schools provides a detailed Privacy Policy, a consent tool and a clear account deletion process. However, because the site is funded by advertising for free users, users should remain careful about cookies, advertising trackers, third-party sharing and the information they provide on the platform.

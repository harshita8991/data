# Practical 3: Regulation Compliance Assessment of W3Schools

## Aim
To examine the privacy regulations applicable to W3Schools and assess how its privacy practices address important legal and regulatory requirements related to personal data protection.

## Organization
W3Schools is an online web development and programming education platform that provides free tutorials, references, exercises, certifications, paid courses, a code editor, website hosting (Spaces) and a mobile app. It collects and processes user information while providing these services, and it is funded by advertising for free users.

## Applicable Privacy Regulations
The major privacy regulations relevant to W3Schools include:

1. **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023** – applicable to processing of digital personal data in India where its requirements apply.
2. **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** – relevant to the processing of personal data of individuals in the EEA, and the UK equivalent where applicable.
3. **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)** – relevant where its applicability requirements are met.

W3Schools' Privacy Policy contains specific sections for the EEA/UK and the United States. These describe rights such as access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and opting out of the sale or sharing of data for advertising. The policy also states that it follows the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. No India-specific section was found in the policy.

## Compliance Assessment

| Requirement | W3Schools Practice | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Data Collection | Describes categories collected: contact, account, transaction, support, device and usage data | Safe |
| Purpose of Processing | States purposes such as operating and improving services, support, security, legal compliance and marketing | Safe |
| Consent | Asks for consent separately through a consent tool for analytics and personalized ads, and allows withdrawal at any time | Safe |
| Data Security | States that administrative, technical and physical safeguards are used, but admits no measure is foolproof | Safe |
| Data Retention | States data is kept for the "minimum period necessary" with no specific periods given | Risky |
| Data Deletion | States that account data is deleted within 30 days after account deletion, subject to exceptions | Safe |
| User Rights | Provides rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent | Safe |
| Third-party Sharing | Describes sharing with sub-processors and advertising partners, some of whom act as independent controllers | Risky |
| International Transfer | Describes processing in the USA or Europe, with safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework | Risky |
| Children's Data | States the service is generally not intended for users under 16 and follows COPPA for under-13s | Risky |
| Regional Coverage | Has EEA/UK and US sections but no dedicated section for Indian users under the DPDP Act | Risky |

> This assessment is based on W3Schools' published Privacy Policy (last updated 16 September 2026).

## Findings
- W3Schools addresses most core requirements: clear collection categories, stated purposes, consent controls, security safeguards and user rights.
- Consent for analytics and personalized advertising is collected separately and can be withdrawn.
- Third-party sharing is a concern because some advertising partners decide independently how they use data.
- Data retention is not defined by specific time periods, except for account deletion.
- Data may be processed outside the user's country, although legal safeguards are described.
- The policy does not include a section dedicated to the DPDP Act, 2023, so Indian users' rights are not explicitly described.
- The under-16 age guidance may not fully match the DPDP Act, which treats individuals under 18 as children.

## Recommendations
- Add a section covering the DPDP Act, 2023, including the rights of Indian users and a grievance contact.
- Publish specific retention periods for each category of personal data.
- Strengthen age verification and describe how verifiable parental consent is handled for children.
- Reduce or clearly limit sharing with advertising partners who act as independent controllers.
- Make the list of sub-processors and advertising partners easier to find and review.
- Continue offering vendor-level consent controls and keep them easy to access.

## Result
The Regulation Compliance Assessment of W3Schools was successfully performed. The assessment examined its practices against the DPDP Act, GDPR and CCPA, and identified areas where its practices meet requirements and areas of risk.

## Conclusion
W3Schools provides a detailed Privacy Policy with region-specific sections, consent controls and user rights that address key requirements of GDPR and CCPA. However, areas such as third-party advertising sharing, international data transfers, vague retention periods, children's data, and the lack of a dedicated DPDP Act section may create compliance risks.
